ENEL 585 Quiz 3 · Side 1: Topic 10 Rectifiers + Lab 3Notation as in the slides: $\theta=\omega t$, $\hat V_g$ = peak of $v_g$ (problems write $V_g$), $X_c=\omega L_c$, $x=X_cI_d/\hat V_g$, $\langle\cdot\rangle$ = average. Show every interval and equation (no working = max half marks).

A. General method for ANY rectifier problem

  1. Model the dc side. $L_d$ large $\Rightarrow i_d=I_d$ (a current source). Check: $X_{L_d}=2\pi f_{\text{rip}}L_d\gg R$? ($f_{\text{rip}}$: half-wave $f$, 1φ bridge $2f$, 3φ bridge $6f$.) If not, $i_d\approx v_d/R$ and $i_g$ is closer to a sinusoid.
  2. Split the cycle ($0$ to $2\pi$ in $\theta$) at: zero crossings of $v_g$, where phases cross (3φ), firing instants $\alpha,\ \pi+\alpha$, end of overlap $\alpha+u$.
  3. Method of assumed states in each interval: guess each device on/off, solve the circuit, then check
    on: $i\ge 0$ ✓   off: $v_{AK}\le 0$ ✓ (diode). Fails → flip it, re-solve.
    Thyristor: turns on only if $v_{AK}>0$ and gated; turns off only when $i\to0$ (so it keeps conducting after $v_g$ reverses: $v_d$ dips below 0).
    With $L_c$: the current in $L_c$ cannot jump, so incoming and outgoing devices are both on (overlap); get $v_d$ from KVL.
  4. Sketch $v_d$ and $i_g$ vs $\theta$; label which devices conduct in each interval.
  5. Average: $\langle v_d\rangle=\frac{1}{\Theta}\int_{\Theta} v_d\,d\theta$ ($\Theta$ = repeat period). Steady state $\langle v_{L_d}\rangle=0\Rightarrow\langle v_d\rangle=V_o+I_dR$.
    $\int_a^b\sin\theta\,d\theta=\cos a-\cos b,\quad \int_a^b\cos\theta\,d\theta=\sin b-\sin a$
  6. Overlap $u$ (volt-seconds that ramp the $L_c$ current): $\displaystyle \omega L_c\,\Delta i_{L_c}\cdot n_L=\int_{\alpha}^{\alpha+u}v_{\text{loop}}\,d\theta$ ($n_L$ = no. of $L_c$ in the commutating loop, $\Delta i$ = change in each).
    Drop: $\Delta\langle v_d\rangle=\dfrac{(\text{commutations per }2\pi)\times(\text{area lost each})}{2\pi}$.
  7. Power: $P=\langle v_d\rangle I_d$ (lossless), $S=V_{g,\text{rms}}I_{g,\text{rms}}$, $k_p=P/S$; $Q$ from the fundamental of $i_g$ (Section D).

C. Phase control, inversion, commutation failure

  • $I_d>0$ always, so $P=\langle v_d\rangle I_d$ has the sign of $\langle v_d\rangle$. $\alpha<90^\circ$: rectifier. $\alpha>90^\circ$: $\langle v_d\rangle<0$, inverter; the dc source must be reversed ($V_o<0$) and supplies the grid.
  • Inversion needs a path that keeps $v_d=v_g$ after $v_g<0$ (an SCR pair). A freewheel diode (half-wave FWD, hybrid bridge) clamps $v_d\ge0$: no inversion.
  • Limit: overlap must end before $\pi$: $\alpha+u<\pi$. Extinction angle $\gamma=\pi-\alpha-u$ (outgoing SCR reverse biased for $\gamma$; must cover turn-off time).
    $\gamma=0$: $\cos\alpha_{\max}=-1+kx$; bridge $\alpha_{\max}=\cos^{-1}(2x-1)$, $\langle v_d\rangle\ge-\frac{2\hat V_g}{\pi}(1-x)$. Then plug $\alpha_{\max}$ into the $\langle v_d\rangle$ row (with drop) for the largest $|\langle v_d\rangle|$.
  • Commutation failure ($\alpha>\alpha_{\max}$): $i_g$ turns back before $-I_d$, outgoing pair stays on, $v_d=v_g$ swings positive; real $L_d$: $L_d\,di_d/dt=v_g+|v_o|$ so $i_d$ climbs. Causes: late firing, dip in $\hat V_g$, rise in $I_d$. Fix: fire earlier, lower $|v_o|$.
  • Overlap is shortest near $\alpha=90^\circ$ ($v_g$ at peak); the drop $2X_cI_d/\pi$ is the same for any $\alpha$.

E. Power factor correction (boost PFC)

  1. Goal: grid sees a resistor, $i_g=v_g/R_e$ ($k_p=1$, THD $\approx0$). $R_e$ is set by control, not a component: $$R_e=\frac{V_{g,\text{rms}}^2}{P}=\frac{\hat V_g^2}{2P},\qquad \hat I_g=\frac{\hat V_g}{R_e}=\frac{2P}{\hat V_g}$$
  2. After the bridge: $i_L=|v_g|/R_e$. $Q_1$ on: $v_L=|v_g|$ (rises); off: $v_L=|v_g|-V_d<0$ (falls). Need $V_d>\hat V_g$, else current is uncontrolled near the line peaks.
  3. Each switching period is quasi steady state ($\langle v_L\rangle_{T_s}\approx0$): $$d(t)=1-\frac{|v_g(t)|}{V_d},\qquad d_{\min}=1-\frac{\hat V_g}{V_d}$$ ($d=1$ at the zero crossings, lowest at the line peak.)
  4. $p=v_gi_g=P(1-\cos2\omega t)$; $C$ absorbs the difference: $v_d$ ripples at $2\omega$, $\Delta v_{d,pp}\approx\frac{P}{\omega CV_d}$.
Totem pole: fast leg $Q_1,Q_2$ at $f_s$, slow leg ($D_1,D_2$ or $Q_3,Q_4$) once per half-cycle; 2 devices in the path vs 3. H-bridge active rectifier: $P$ either way, can supply/absorb $Q$; unipolar ripple at $2f_s$ (smaller $L$) but all 4 switch.

1. Averaged → small-signal model (any converter)

  1. Two CCM states: write $v_L$ and $i_C$ with $Q_1$ on ($dT_s$) and off ($d'T_s$).
  2. Average (signals → $\langle\cdot\rangle_{T_s}$): $L\frac{d\langle i_L\rangle}{dt}=d\,v_{L,\text{on}}+d'\,v_{L,\text{off}}$,  $C\frac{d\langle v\rangle}{dt}=d\,i_{C,\text{on}}+d'\,i_{C,\text{off}}$. Products $d\langle x\rangle$ make it nonlinear.
  3. Operating point: set $d/dt=0$ (volt-second, charge balance) → $V$, $I_L$.
  4. Perturb: $\langle x\rangle=X+\hat x$, $d=D+\hat d$, $d'=D'-\hat d$. Expand; dc terms cancel; drop $\hat d\,\hat x$ (small × small, since $|\hat x|\ll|X|$, $|\hat d|\ll D,D'$).
  5. Matrix: $\dfrac{d\hat{\mathbf x}}{dt}=\mathbf A\hat{\mathbf x}+\mathbf B\hat{\mathbf u}$, $\hat{\mathbf x}=\begin{bmatrix}\hat i_L\\ \hat v\end{bmatrix}$, $\hat{\mathbf u}=\begin{bmatrix}\hat v_g\\ \hat d\end{bmatrix}$ (row 1 ÷ $L$, row 2 ÷ $C$).
  6. Solve $\hat{\mathbf x}(s)=(s\mathbf I-\mathbf A)^{-1}\mathbf B\hat{\mathbf u}$. For $\mathbf A=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&e\end{bmatrix}$: $\Delta(s)=s^2-(a+e)s+(ae-bc)$, $(s\mathbf I-\mathbf A)^{-1}=\frac{1}{\Delta}\begin{bmatrix}s-e&b\\c&s-a\end{bmatrix}$ so $\hat v=\dfrac{c\,(\mathbf B_1\hat{\mathbf u})+(s-a)(\mathbf B_2\hat{\mathbf u})}{\Delta(s)}$.
    (Or by hand: Laplace each equation, solve row 1 for $\hat i_L$, substitute.)
  7. Standard form: divide by the constant term of $\Delta$: $\omega_0^2=ae-bc$, $\ \omega_0/Q=-(a+e)$: $$G(s)=G_0\frac{1\pm s/\omega_z}{1+\frac{s}{Q\omega_0}+\left(\frac{s}{\omega_0}\right)^2}\quad (1-s/\omega_z:\ \text{RHP zero})$$ $G_0=G(0)$ = steady-state slope ($dV/dD$ or $V/V_g$).

3. Feedback loop and stability

$$T=\pm\frac{H\,G_c\,G_{vd}}{V_M}\;\big(\times e^{-s\tau}\big)$$ ($+$ if $G_{d0}>0$; $-$ for an inverting converter with the $-1$ block; $\times e^{-s\tau}$ for a digital delay).
Regulated output: $Hv=v_{\text{ref}}\Rightarrow V=V_{\text{ref}}/H$ (inverting: $-V_{\text{ref}}/H$). $$\hat v_e=\frac{\hat v_{\text{ref}}}{1+T},\qquad \frac{\hat v}{\hat v_g}=\frac{G_{vg}}{1+T}$$ $$\frac{\hat v}{\hat v_{\text{ref}}}=\frac1H\frac{T}{1+T}\approx\begin{cases}1/H,&|T|\gg1\\ T/H,&|T|\ll1\end{cases}$$
  • Crossover $f_c$: $|T(f_c)|=1$. Phase margin $\varphi_m=180^\circ+\angle T(f_c)$. Stable if $T$ has no RHP poles, $|T|$ crosses 1 once, and $\varphi_m>0$. At $f_c$: $|1+T|=2\sin(\varphi_m/2)$.
  • Steady-state error (step of $v_{\text{ref}}$): $1/(1+T(0))$. Integrator in $G_c$ → $|T(0)|\to\infty$ → zero error.
  • Overshoot ($0<\varphi_m<90^\circ$): $Q\approx\dfrac{\sqrt{\cos\varphi_m}}{\sin\varphi_m}$, $\zeta=\dfrac{1}{2Q}$, $\%\text{OS}=100\,e^{-\pi\zeta/\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}}$. $\varphi_m\ge76^\circ\Rightarrow Q\le0.5$: no overshoot. (52°: $Q\approx1$, 16%; 45°: 23%.)
  • $f_c\ll f_s$ (e.g. $f_s/20$) so $|T(f_s)|\ll1$ and $1/V_M$ holds; higher $f_c$ = faster but less phase left against the resonance/RHP zero/delay.

4. Compensator design steps (PI)

  1. Uncompensated $T_u=HG_{vd}/V_M$ ($G_c=1$): $T_u(0)=HG_{d0}/V_M$, resonant peak $\approx Q\,T_u(0)$ at $f_0$. Constant $G_c$ fails: peak $<1$ forces $|T(0)|<1$ (large error); large $G_c$ crosses past $-180^\circ$ (unstable).
  2. PI: $G_c=G_{cm}\left(1+\dfrac{\omega_L}{s}\right)$. Below $f_L$: integrator ($-20$ dB/dec, $-90^\circ$); above: flat $G_{cm}$, $0^\circ$.
  3. Put $f_L$ below the lowest $f_0$ (higher $f_L$: more low-$f$ gain, but PI adds $\sqrt{1+(f_L/f_0)^2}$ at the resonance).
  4. Set $G_{cm}$ so the peak $|T(f_0)|\approx G_{cm}\,Q\,T_u(0)\sqrt{1+(f_L/f_0)^2}\le0.5$ ($-6$ dB) at the worst corner.
  5. Crossover (below $f_L$ and $f_0$): $T\approx G_{cm}T_u(0)\omega_L/s\Rightarrow f_c\approx G_{cm}T_u(0)f_L$. Pure integrator $G_c=\omega_I/s$: $f_c\approx\frac{\omega_I T_u(0)}{2\pi}$.
  6. $\varphi_m=180^\circ+\angle T(f_c)$, with $\angle T=-90^\circ+\tan^{-1}\!\frac{f_c}{f_L}+\angle_{\text{quad}}-\tan^{-1}\!\frac{f_c}{f_z}-360^\circ f_c\tau$.
  7. Check: $|T(10\text{ Hz})|\approx G_{cm}T_u(0)f_L/(10)$ (rejection), $|T(f_s)|\ll1$, every operating point (new $D$ → new $G_{d0},f_0,Q,f_z$).

Feedforward: $d=d_{ff}+v_c/V_M$ (one PWM: compare $V_Md_{ff}+v_c$). $d_{ff}$ from ideal $M(D)$ with $V=V_{\text{ref}}/H$: buck $\frac{v_{\text{ref}}}{Hv_g}$, boost $1-\frac{H\langle v_g\rangle}{v_{\text{ref}}}$, buck-boost $\frac{v_{\text{ref}}}{v_{\text{ref}}+H\langle v_g\rangle}$. Acts before an error builds; does not change $T$; feedback still needed (losses, load, model error). Low-pass a $v_{\text{ref}}$ step (a duty step rings the LC resonance above $f_c$); don't over-filter measured $v_g$ (delays the correction).

6. Controlling an ac current (inverter, PFC)

  1. Reference ($i_L$ + toward grid; $Q_g$ = vars absorbed by grid; "grid supplies $Q$" → $Q_g<0$): $i_P^*=\dfrac{2P^*}{\hat V_g},\ i_Q^*=-\dfrac{2Q_g^*}{\hat V_g},\ i^*=i_P^*\sin\theta+i_Q^*\cos\theta$.  $a\sin\theta+b\cos\theta=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\sin(\theta+\operatorname{atan2}(b,a))$.
  2. Plant: $L\frac{d\langle i_L\rangle}{dt}=m\langle v_d\rangle-\langle v_g\rangle$ (bipolar $m=2d-1$, $\langle v_t\rangle=mv_d$). Small-signal $G_p=\hat i_L/\hat v_L=1/(sL)$.
  3. Feedforward: $v_{L,ff}=L\frac{di^*}{dt}=\omega_1L(i_P^*\cos\theta-i_Q^*\sin\theta)$; $v_t^*=v_{g,ff}+v_{L,ff}+v_c$; $m=v_t^*/v_d$, need $|m|\le1$ → $V_d^*>$ peak of $v_t^*$ (+ ripple margin). Compensate delay: evaluate at $\theta+\omega_1\tau$.
  4. Loop: $T=G_c\,e^{-s\tau}/(sL)$. P-only: $\boxed{K_p=2\pi f_cL}$, $\varphi_m=90^\circ-360^\circ f_c\tau$.
  5. Residual 60 Hz voltage error $D$ across $L$ (e.g. grid FF not advanced: $|D|\approx\omega_1\tau\hat V_g$) → current error $\approx|D|/|G_c(j\omega_1)|$. PR: $G_c=K_p+K_r\frac{2\omega_bs}{s^2+2\omega_bs+\omega_1^2}$, $|G_c(j\omega_1)|=K_p+K_r$ → error $\div(1+K_r/K_p)$; band $\approx f_1\pm f_b$; ideal PR: infinite gain at $f_1$.
Boost PFC current loop: $i^*=\hat I^*|\sin\theta|$, $\hat I^*=2P/\hat V_g$; $v_{L,ff}=\omega_1L\hat I^*\cos\theta$ ($0<\theta<\pi$, max $\pm\omega_1L\hat I^*$ at crossings); $\boxed{d=1-\frac{|v_g|-v_L^*}{v_d}}$, $v_L^*=v_{L,ff}+v_c$. Zero crossing: even $d=1$ gives only $v_L=|v_g|\approx\hat V_g\theta<\omega_1L\hat I^*$ for $\theta<\omega_1L\hat I^*/\hat V_g$; holding $d=1$: $i=\frac{\hat V_g}{\omega_1L}(1-\cos\theta)\approx\frac{\hat V_g\theta^2}{2\omega_1L}$ meets $\hat I^*\theta$ at $\theta=2\omega_1L\hat I^*/\hat V_g$. Uses PI (rectified reference has no 60 Hz for a resonant term).
DC-link voltage loop (outer): $Cv_d\frac{dv_d}{dt}=p_d-P\ \Rightarrow\ \boxed{\dfrac{\hat v_d}{\hat p^*}=-\dfrac{1}{CV_ds}}$ (negative integrator). Error $e_v=v_d-V_d^*$ (measured − ref) cancels the minus: $T_v=\dfrac{G_{cv}}{CV_ds}$, $G_{cv}=G_{cvm}\left(1+\frac{\omega_{Lv}}{s}\right)$.
  1. $f_{co}\ll120$ Hz (≈8 to 10 Hz); $f_{Lv}=f_{co}/5$.
  2. $|T_v(f_{co})|=1$: $\ G_{cvm}=\dfrac{2\pi f_{co}CV_d}{\sqrt{1+(f_{Lv}/f_{co})^2}}$; $\ \varphi_m=90^\circ-\tan^{-1}\frac{f_{Lv}}{f_{co}}$ ($=78.7^\circ$ for 1/5).
  3. Check $|T_v(120)|\ll1$; current loop $f_c\ge10f_{co}$ → treat it as 1 ($P=P^*$).
Why slow: $p_g=P(1-\cos2\theta)$ → 120 Hz ripple on $v_d$; a fast loop puts it in $i_P^*$, and $\times\sin\theta$ makes 180 Hz (3rd) + 60 Hz in $i^*$. Step up in $p_d$: $v_d$ rises, PI raises $P^*$, $P$ catches $p_d$ at the $v_d$ peak, then $P>p_d$ returns $v_d$ to $V_d^*$; final $i_P^*=2p_d/\hat V_g$.

Traps to check before you hand in

  • Peak vs rms: $\hat V=\sqrt2V_{\text{rms}}$; 3φ phase peak $=\sqrt2V_{LL}/\sqrt3$. $X_c=2\pi fL_c$; angles in rad inside $\cos^{-1}$, $u=\omega t_u$.
  • $\langle v_d\rangle$ with $L_c$: drop applies at every $\alpha$; inversion needs $V_o<0$ and $\alpha+u<\pi$.
  • $\omega$ (rad/s) vs $f$ (Hz): $G_c=250/s$ has $s$ in rad/s; $f_c=\omega_c/2\pi$.
  • Inverting converter: $G_{d0}<0$ → use the $-1$ block so $T$ is positive at dc.
  • $\varphi_m$ from asymptotes is an estimate: also add the quadratic's and RHP zero's lag at $f_c$ and $360^\circ f_c\tau$.
  • $Q_g$ sign (absorbed by the grid); dc-link error is $v_d-V_d^*$; check $|m|\le1$ and $d\in[0,1]$.
  • Write the interval, the equation set up, then the number (half marks without working).
