2. Assignments 5 and 6
Posted: Oct 21, 2026, 4:21 PM
----------------------------------------------------------------------
On Assignment 5 there was a question about the ratio of impedances and it has been causing a lot of problems for many of you. It is a rare example where if you use extra sig digs than Achieve is expecting (in this case 2) then your unrounded value will fall outside the 2% tolerance window. This cost many of you a lot of attempts so to fix this, I will take Assignment 5 as being out of one less question and cap the grades at 100%.

On Assignment 6 there is a question about a clarinet and its 3rd normal mode. Please note the subtle language use here: normal modes refer to sequential harmonic frequencies, so the first, second, and third normal modes for a clarinet correspond to the 1st, 3rd, and 5th harmonics. On an exam we will not use the phrase "normal modes" but instead refer to harmonics to avoid this type of confusion.

Posted: Oct 13, 2026, 8:58 AM
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The midterm exam will cover material from Weeks 1-6 in the course schedule, which can be found under Content/Course Information. This includes all material up to and including resonant modes of air columns. The midterm in the previous years was a little later in the semester, so they include Doppler shift and beating frequencies, but these topics will not be covered on this year's midterm.

The exam will be 20 multiple choice questions with a range of difficulty levels and a mixture of calculation and theoretical questions, similar to the 2024 and 2025 midterms.

8. Reminder on Use of AI in this course
Posted: Sep 14, 2026, 7:00 AM
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Hi Everyone,

As generative AI and LLMs are tools that are increasingly being integrated into professional
life, this course allows the use of AI to help students prepare lab reports, in whole or in
part. It is the student's responsibility to ensure that the information provided by the AI tool
used is accurate, at an appropriate level for the course, consistent with the methods and
notation being used in the course, and free of any AI generation errors, such as non-existent
references. If AI tools are used, it should be declared on the cover page of the lab report,
stating which tools and how they were used.
When completing homework problems, the homework platform Achieve has a built-in AI
chatbot tutor that students are encouraged to use when they get stuck. Students should not
use additional AI tools to complete their homework.

This policy is also available in the course outline.

Thanks,

Tim
