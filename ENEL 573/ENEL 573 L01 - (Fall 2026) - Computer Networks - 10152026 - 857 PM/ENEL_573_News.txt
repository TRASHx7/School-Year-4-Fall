3. Midterm - Clarification on Formulas
Posted: Oct 21, 2026, 11:08 AM
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Hi everyone,

I would like to clarify a few things regarding the formulas for the upcoming midterm exam.

First, there are not many "complex formulas" in this course that you need to memorize. In particular, for Chapter 2 (Network Analysis), you are not expected to memorize the detailed derivations. Instead, you should understand the key concepts and derivations, and be familiar with the important conclusions, especially the performance analysis results for the M/M/1 queue covered in class. We have introduced the detailed analysis for this type of queueing ONLY.

As a general guideline, please refer to the homework assignments for the level of understanding and familiarity with formulas expected for the midterm. You should be comfortable with the formulas needed to solve problems at a similar level.

As I have mentioned to some students who asked the same question, any "complex formulas" that I consider necessary for the exam will be provided during the exam.

I hope this helps clarify expectations and address any concerns about memorizing formulas.

Thanks,
John
